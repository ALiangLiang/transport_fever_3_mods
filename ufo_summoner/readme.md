> **Version**: 1.0.0 (Revision 1)

---

## Overview

Transport Fever 3 features an official UFO Easter Egg and the exclusive achievement **"Encounter of the 3rd Kind"** (`ach_ufo`). In the vanilla game, the UFO appears randomly only once every few in-game decades.

This mod provides a dedicated **UFO Summoner Control Panel**:
1. **Top-Left Quick Button**: Click the **UFO** button in the top-left corner of the game screen to open the control window.
2. **On-Demand Official Summon**: Immediately triggers the official UFO easter egg without waiting decades.
3. **Airspace Radar Tracker**: Real-time detection of active UFOs, target cattle entities, and flight phases (Approaching, Targeting Cow, Abducting Cow!, Leaving Airspace, Despawning).
4. **Smooth Camera Tracking**: One-click smooth camera tracking for either the UFO entity or the target cow.
5. **Achievement Helper**: Essential for completing the "Encounter of the 3rd Kind" achievement.

---

## How to Use

1. **Open the Control Window**:
   - Click the **UFO** button in the top-left corner of the game screen.
2. **Summon a UFO**:
   - Click **"Summon UFO"** in the header or **"Summon Here"** next to any detected cow pasture in the "Livestock Farms" tab.
   - *Requirement*: Official UFO mechanics require at least one Livestock Farm with cattle on the map.
3. **Track & Observe**:
   - Click **"Follow UFO"** or **"Follow Cow"** to smoothly focus and follow the action.
   - *Tip*: Click directly on the UFO entity in the game world to unlock the achievement!
